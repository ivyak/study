原典 p.1・定理2

定理2｜期待値の性質（離散型）

① \(\operatorname{E}(aX+b)=a\operatorname{E}(X)+b\)

\[ \begin{aligned} \operatorname{E}(aX+b) &=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(ax_n+b)f_X(x_n)\\ &=a\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}x_nf_X(x_n)+b\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}f_X(x_n)\\ &=a\operatorname{E}(X)+b. \end{aligned} \]

② \(\operatorname{E}(X+Y)=\operatorname{E}(X)+\operatorname{E}(Y)\)

\[ \varphi(x,y)=x+y,\qquad U=\varphi(X,Y)=X+Y. \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \operatorname{E}(U) &=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\varphi(x_n,y_m)f_{X,Y}(x_n,y_m)\\ \operatorname{E}(X+Y) &=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}(x_n+y_m)f_{X,Y}(x_n,y_m). \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \operatorname{E}(X+Y) &=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}(x_n+y_m)f_{X,Y}(x_n,y_m)\\ &=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}x_n\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}f_{X,Y}(x_n,y_m) +\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}y_m\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}f_{X,Y}(x_n,y_m)\\ &=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}x_nf_X(x_n)+\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}y_mf_Y(y_m)\\ &=\operatorname{E}(X)+\operatorname{E}(Y). \end{aligned} \]

③ \(\operatorname{E}(XY)=\operatorname{E}(X)\operatorname{E}(Y)\)

\[ \varphi(x,y)=xy,\qquad U=\varphi(X,Y)=XY. \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \operatorname{E}(U) &=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\varphi(x_n,y_m)f_{X,Y}(x_n,y_m)\\ \operatorname{E}(XY) &=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}x_ny_mf_{X,Y}(x_n,y_m). \end{aligned} \]
\[ X\perp\!\!\!\perp Y\quad\Longrightarrow\quad f_{X,Y}(x_n,y_m)=f_X(x_n)f_Y(y_m). \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \operatorname{E}(XY) &=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}x_ny_mf_{X,Y}(x_n,y_m)\\ &=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}x_ny_mf_X(x_n)f_Y(y_m)\\ &=\left(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}x_nf_X(x_n)\right) \left(\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}y_mf_Y(y_m)\right)\\ &=\operatorname{E}(X)\operatorname{E}(Y). \end{aligned} \]
